About
Dr. Jayasri Kannan

Dr. Jayasri Kannan is a nursing professional, educator, researcher, and healthcare advocate with 9 years of professional experience. She holds a Ph.D. in Nursing and an M.Sc. in Nursing (Gold Medalist, SRM IST), serving as Assistant Professor & Research Supervisor at Tantia University.

Through Jayasri Kannan Foundation, her extensive background in healthcare, clinical practice, research, and health awareness forms the bedrock for initiatives dedicated to community service, health education, and social empowerment.

Welcome

Welcome to
Jayasri Kannan Foundation

Jayasri Kannan Foundation is a charitable organisation committed to serving communities through meaningful social welfare initiatives.

We work to identify community needs and support programmes in healthcare, eye care, blood donation, education, youth welfare and rural community development.

Our aim is to bring people, professionals, institutions and volunteers together to create positive and sustainable change.

Our Vision

To create a healthy, educated, empowered, and socially developed society by supporting people in need.

Our Mission

To provide healthcare, education, social welfare, environmental protection, and development support to poor, rural, vulnerable, and underserved communities.
